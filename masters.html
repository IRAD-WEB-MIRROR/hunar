Masters

Twelve of our twenty-two masters. Most sell from home: ring before you go. The others can be reached through hunar@korkom.org.

Felt · Talas Alatau (ATR)

Zarina Ashyrbek kyzy

Shyrdak carpets; the Palace of Art bought her Tört mezgil in 2016. Two apprentices from Kara-Koyun.

Yurt · ATR

Janyl Kadyrkul kyzy

Head of a yurt-making family workshop whose tündük crowns are sold across Central Asia.

Saddlery · Kyzyl-Adyr

Asylbek Toktomushev

Decorated wooden saddles with bone and silver inlay. Six saddles a year, each to order.

Felt · Aksay Plateau

Gulnara Kurmanbekova

Felt saddlecloths and ala-kiyiz in the old Aksay colours: madder, walnut and indigo.

Embroidery · Talas

Aiperi Sadykova

Köküröktük chest panels and cuffs in silk chain and cross stitch.

Pottery · Sopol-Tepa, Lower Naryn

Rustam Nazarov

Slip-painted bowls from the old kilns of Sopol-Tepa, fired with walnut wood.

Metal · Coppersmiths' Row, Talas

Bolot Isakov

Copper jugs and trays, raised by hand in the workshop his grandfather opened in 1927.

Instruments · Talas

Erkin Mamyrov

Komuz and kyl-kyak from apricot and juniper wood; twenty instruments a year.

Yurt · Uzun-Akmat valley

Kubanych Abdyrakhmanov

Lattice walls and roof poles in willow, bent over a fire.

Saddlery · Toktogul

Nurlan Sharshenov

Bridles, cruppers and breastplates in leather and silver; restores old saddles for the Palace.

Metal · Kara-Buura

Daniyar Kenesh uulu

Horn-handled knives forged from old lorry springs.

Pottery · Tash-Kömür

Mahbuba Yusupova

Green-glazed plov dishes and the big lagman bowls of the Lower Naryn teahouses.
